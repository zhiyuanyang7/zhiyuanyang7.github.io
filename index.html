About

  • I am a Golomb Visiting Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Purdue University. My research interests are in free probability and operator algebras. My mentor is Thomas Sinclair. I received my Ph.D. from Texas A&M University in 2026 under the supervision of Michael Anshelevich.
